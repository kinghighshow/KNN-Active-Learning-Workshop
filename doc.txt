DATA
----
- 8,760 hourly records for Waterloo, ON (2025) from the Open-Meteo Historical Weather API:
  https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/historical-weather-api
- Stored in the Neon PostgreSQL table knn_weather. A cached copy is kept in data/waterloo_weather_2025.csv.
- Target: the WMO weather_code grouped into four classes:
    Clear  = codes 0-1
    Cloudy = codes 2-3
    Rain   = codes 51-67, 80-82
    Snow   = codes 71-77, 85-86
- Features: temperature, relative humidity, dew point, apparent temperature, surface pressure,
  cloud cover, wind speed, wind direction, wind gusts, hour and month.
- weather_code, rain, snowfall and precipitation are left out of the features to avoid leakage,
  because the target is built from them.


PREPROCESSING
-------------
- Extracted hour and month from the timestamp.
- Median imputer and StandardScaler, both fitted on the training set only.
- Stratified 80/20 train/test split.


MODEL
-----
- scikit-learn KNeighborsClassifier.
- k (3-25) and the distance metric (Euclidean or Manhattan) are tuned with 5-fold
  cross-validation on the training set, scored by macro F1.


RESULTS
-------
- Best k = 3 (Euclidean).
- Test set: 86.7% accuracy, 0.804 macro F1.
- Majority-class baseline: 45.1% accuracy, 0.155 macro F1.
- Rain and Snow are the hardest classes and are mostly confused with Cloudy.
- Removing cloud_cover drops accuracy to 74.9%, almost all of it in Clear vs Cloudy.
- With whole weeks held out, macro F1 is about 0.61. We treat this as the realistic
  estimate for weeks the model has never seen.


ARCHITECTURE (ML PIPELINE PATTERN)
----------------------------------
DataLoader -> Preprocessor -> KNNModel -> Evaluator, chained by KNNPipeline.
